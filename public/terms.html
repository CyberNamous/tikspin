1. About TikSpin

TikSpin is a free platform where TikTok streamers (the “host”) run spin-the-wheel giveaways for Roblox and Mobile Legends: Bang Bang. We, the platform operator, provide the tools and run the random draw. We do not organize, fund, sponsor or deliver any giveaway or prize. Each giveaway belongs to the host who runs it.

2. Who can use TikSpin

  • You must be at least 13 years old, and old enough to use TikTok and the game where you live. If you are under 18, ask a parent or guardian before you use TikSpin or enter a giveaway.
  • If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 13, we may remove it. A parent or guardian can also email us to ask for removal.
  • TikSpin is available worldwide, and the laws where you live apply to you. We do not promise the service is available or suitable in every country.

3. Accounts

  • One person, one account. Using several accounts, or entering for someone else, is not allowed.
  • You choose a display username and a password, and give your TikTok @username. Your display username is unique and is the only name shown publicly, for example on the wheel. Your TikTok @username is private: only the host of a giveaway you enter, and the operator, can see it.
  • When you create your account you receive a 6-digit recovery PIN, shown once. Keep your password and PIN safe and never share them with anyone, including us. We will never ask you for them.
  • You are responsible for everything done with your account. Too many wrong password or PIN tries may lock sign-in or recovery for a while.
  • You can change your display username or TikTok @username with your password and PIN, with a waiting period between changes. Earlier names may be kept on record so that bans and conflict checks still work.
  • If you lose your PIN, you can ask the operator for help by email. We check each request by hand and may ask for proof that the account is yours.

4. Joining a giveaway

You can only join a giveaway with the code the host shares. Fill in what the giveaway asks for (your Roblox username, or your Mobile Legends IGN, User ID and Server ID). Each account can enter a giveaway once. Some giveaways ask for a screenshot showing that you follow the host on TikTok. Entering is always free, and you will never be asked to pay to enter or to claim a prize.

5. Honest entries

Give true details. Use your own Roblox username and your own Mobile Legends account. Do not edit, fake or reuse screenshots, and do not use tricks to get extra entries.

  • If the same game account is entered by two different TikSpin accounts, both entries are put on hold. The host reviews them and decides. This is not a ban.
  • Hosts can see when entries come from the same device. This is only a hint for the host, and it never blocks or bans anyone automatically.

6. Winners and prizes

  • The wheel picks at random from the players on it. The host controls who is on the wheel and starts each spin. The host’s decisions about entries, disqualifications and results within their own giveaway are final.
  • The host decides what the prize is, who provides it, and how and when it is delivered. TikSpin does not hold, buy, deliver or guarantee any prize and is not responsible if a prize is not delivered. Prizes have no cash value unless the host says so.
  • If you win, the host will contact you on TikTok and may use the game details you entered to deliver the prize. If you do not respond in time, or your details are wrong, the prize may go to someone else or may not be deliverable. Questions about a prize go to the host.

7. Disqualification and Permanent Bans

  • Disqualification is decided by the host and applies to that one giveaway only. A host may also keep their own list of people they do not want in their giveaways.
  • A Permanent Ban applies across the whole platform. Only the operator can give one, and only after reports from several different hosts with clear evidence, reviewed by hand. It covers the display username, TikTok @usernames (current and earlier) and game accounts involved.
  • If you believe a ban is a mistake, email us. We will review it, and we can reverse it.

8. Screenshots and content

Only submit screenshots and details that are yours and true. Do not upload anything unlawful, harmful or that shows other people’s private information. Follow screenshots are used only so the host can review your entry, and are deleted once the host decides.

9. Your data

How we collect, use, share and protect your personal data is explained in our Privacy Policy. By using TikSpin you confirm you have read it.

10. Optional support

Nothing is deleted until the review is finished.

  • Register with your own TikTok account and give your TikTok username and display name accurately. Players use them to find you.
  • Keep your password and recovery PIN safe. You are responsible for everything done with your account.
  • If you think your account was taken over, email the operator at once. After the operator checks the case by hand, the account is deleted and you may register again. This carries no penalty.

3. Your giveaways

  • You can run one giveaway at a time. The requirements you choose when you make the link are locked once it is live. You can close entries, revoke the code, end or delete a giveaway at any time.
  • You are responsible for what you show and say on your stream.

You can see entrants’ TikTok @usernames and entry details only to run your giveaway and deliver prizes. Do not share, sell, publish or misuse them, and do not contact entrants for unrelated purposes. Show only display usernames on stream. Handle entrant data in line with our Privacy Policy.

You check entries yourself, including follows, same-device hints and name conflicts. TikSpin does not verify follows or identities, and it never decides these for you. You decide disqualifications and reinstatements fairly and in good faith. Within your giveaway, your decisions are final.

6. Reports

You may report players from your own giveaways with honest details and evidence. The operator reviews reports by hand and decides on Permanent Bans. False or malicious reports can lead to suspension.

7. Homepage and scheduled giveaways

Showing a giveaway on the homepage is your choice. Scheduled giveaways must be accurate and use plain text without links. The operator may hide any card that is misleading or inappropriate.

8. The platform’s role

TikSpin only provides tools. We are not the organizer or sponsor of your giveaway and are not responsible for your prizes, delivery, disputes with entrants, or any loss that results from your giveaway.

9. Ending

You can delete your giveaways at any time and ask the operator to delete your account. The operator may suspend or remove an account that breaks these terms or the Global Terms.
