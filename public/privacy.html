1. Who this covers

This policy applies to everyone who uses TikSpin, including players and streamers, anywhere in the world. TikSpin is run by the platform operator (“we”). It should be read together with our Terms of Use.

2. What we collect

  • Account details: display username, TikTok @username, and a securely scrambled password and recovery PIN.
  • Entry details: the game details a giveaway asks for, such as a Roblox username, or a Mobile Legends IGN, User ID and Server ID.
  • Screenshots and reports: a follow screenshot if you ask for manual review, and evidence a host attaches to a report.
  • Messages: support requests and anonymous feedback you send us.
  • Technical data: a one-way device code and limited records needed to keep the platform secure and working.

We do not collect your real name, payment details or game passwords.

3. Why we use it

We use your data to run the platform: to create and secure accounts, run giveaways and the random draw, let hosts review entries and contact winners, prevent duplicate or abusive entries, handle support, resets and reports, and meet legal obligations. We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising.

4. Who can see it

  • Everyone: your display username only, for example on the wheel.
  • The host of a giveaway you enter: your TikTok @username and entry details for that giveaway, to run it and deliver prizes.
  • The operator: only to run the platform, handle resets, reports and support, and meet legal obligations.
  • Authorities: only where the law requires, or on a valid legal request.

5. Screenshots and evidence

Your follow screenshot is read on your own device. It is only sent to us if you ask for a manual review, and it is deleted as soon as the host decides. Evidence attached to a report is deleted once the operator decides the case.

6. Service providers

TikSpin is delivered through third-party hosting and network providers. To deliver and protect the service, they may process and temporarily keep technical connection data, such as IP addresses, under their own privacy practices. We do not use it to identify you or for advertising. We store only a one-way device code, not your IP address. The device code is still personal data. We use it to spot duplicate or abusive entries and to limit repeated wrong tries.

7. Keeping and deleting

We keep data only as long as it is needed for the purposes above, then delete it. Entry data is removed after a giveaway ends. Feedback is anonymous and deleted after a limited time. Safety and moderation records, such as report details, are kept only as long as needed. Your account is kept while it is active, and you can ask us to delete it at any time.

8. Children

TikSpin is for people 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, email us and we will remove it. Users under 18 should ask a parent or guardian before using TikSpin.

9. Your choices and rights

  • You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal data by emailing the contact email on the TikSpin homepage. We may ask you to prove the account is yours. If your account is deleted, you will need to register again to enter.
  • We handle personal data in line with applicable laws. For users in the Philippines, this includes the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173). You may also raise concerns with the National Privacy Commission.

10. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures, such as hashed credentials and keeping each host’s data separate. No online service can promise perfect security, so please keep your password and PIN private.

11. Changes and contact

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do. Questions or requests: the contact email on the TikSpin homepage.
